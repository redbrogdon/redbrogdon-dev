# Flutter Social Copy Reference & Guidance

This reference file contains product-specific guidelines and channel DNA for generating social media copy for official **Flutter** channels.

---

## 1. Channel DNA & Brand Voice

* **Handle / Tagging**: `@FlutterDev` on X, `Flutter` on LinkedIn.
* **Tone**: Energetic, visual-heavy, community-oriented ("Builder's Companion").
* **Key Hashtags & Tags**: `#Flutter`, `#FlutterDev`, `#Dart`.
* **Emoji Conventions**:
  * Primary Flutter Emoji: 🩵 (Blue heart)
  * Feature/Release Emojis: 📣 (Announcements), 🚀 (Performance/Momentum), 🛠️ (DevTools/IDE), 💥 (Milestones), 🎨 (UI/UX)

---

## 2. Category Blueprints

### A. Releases & Hotfixes
* **Hook**: 📣 *Flutter X.Y is here!* or *A new Flutter beta just dropped: X.Y!* 🩵
* **Content**: 2-3 key highlights (Impeller, Web, iOS/Android features, DevTools).
* **CTA**: Direct link to release notes or blog post via `[goo.gle link placeholder: <URL>]`.

### B. App / Showcase Spotlights
* **Hook**: 🎨 *Check out what developers are building with Flutter!*
* **Content**: Celebrate creative cross-platform UI, community projects, or enterprise adoption.
* **CTA**: Link to GitHub repo, video demo, or showcase article.
