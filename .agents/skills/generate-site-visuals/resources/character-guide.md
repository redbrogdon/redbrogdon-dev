# Character Design Guide: Bumpy, Spring & Stacks

This guide defines the canonical visual anatomy, palette, and narrative personalities for the recurring editorial character trio on `redbrogdon.dev`.

---

## 1. The Ensemble

### Bumpy
* **Role:** The Thinker & Observer (analytical planner, cautious reviewer, monitors system boundaries and blueprints).
* **Silhouette:** Large bulbous pear/gumdrop shape in textured slate teal (`#527982` / `#628c97`).
* **Key Features:**
  * Small tactile cut-paper lumps and bumps across his body surface (**never band-aids**).
  * **Always wears round wire spectacles** perched on his nose.
* **Limbs:** Paper arms, legs, and **bare feet** (no shoes unless specifically relevant to the topic).
* **Expressions:** Observant, studious, curious, and thoughtful; cautious or worried when things get precarious.

### Spring
* **Role:** The Catalyst & Energy (optimistic, rapid executor, leaps into action, turns cranks, catches errors mid-flight).
* **Silhouette:** **Single contiguous column of spiral coils** in warm terracotta cut paper (`#c2654a` / `#b35338`). May be arced or compressed in motion, but **never separated into multiple broken coils**.
* **Key Features:**
  * **Smooth domed head** atop the coil with friendly, expressive eyes and an open, cheerful smile.
  * **Zero metal embellishments**—he is a pure, tactile papercraft spring.
* **Limbs:** Paper arms, legs, and **bare feet** (no shoes unless specifically relevant to the topic).
* **Expressions:** Enthusiastic, laughing, leaping, and wide-eyed with curiosity.

### Stacks
* **Role:** The Curious Learner (eager apprentice, willing to try ambitious things, occasionally gets in over his head or wobbles).
* **Silhouette:** Exactly **three smooth river pebbles stacked vertically**:
  * **Top Stone:** Blue (`#4a7b9d`)
  * **Middle Stone:** Yellow / Amber gold (`#e5a93b`)
  * **Bottom Stone:** Red / Terracotta (`#c24136`)
* **Key Features:**
  * **All three stone shapes have their own distinct eyes and mouth.** Their three faces can react in unison (e.g. laughing, concentrating, or sleeping) or express complementary comic reactions.
* **Limbs (Strict Rule):**
  * Legs with **bare feet** extend **only from the bottom red stone** (no shoes).
  * Arms extend **only from the middle yellow stone**.
  * No extra arms or legs on the blue stone or elsewhere.
* **Expressions:** Eager, proud, determined, sheepish when tangled, peacefully sleeping.

---

## 2. Art Style: "Literary Interface Curations"

All illustrative assets must strictly conform to this editorial craft style:
* **Medium:** Contemporary pochoir stencil and matte cut-paper collage.
* **Background:** Warm unbleached parchment / paper (`#fbf9f4`).
* **Tactile Depth:** Visible cut-paper edges with soft, warm physical drop shadows giving tangible multi-layer relief.
* **Surface Quality:** Stippled gouache texture and delicate spray-grain gradients across paper cutouts.
* **Zero Typography / Wordless:** No rendered text, numbers, or pseudo-code labels within the illustration canvas. Visual storytelling must be entirely graphical.
* **Anti-Slop Constraints:**
  * Zero glossy 3D CGI or plastic polymer rendering.
  * Zero cyberpunk, neon glows, or high-tech lens flares.
  * Zero generic "Corporate Memphis" flat geometric blob humans.
* **Inclusivity & Cultural Variety:**
  * Seamlessly weave in authentic non-Western everyday craft objects, foods, and textiles when portraying shared moments (e.g. multi-tiered brass tiffin carriers, cutting-chai glasses, samosas, woven kilim rugs, ceramic lanterns).

## 3. Standard Character Prompt Snippet

When calling the image generation tool, combine the scene description with this standardized prompt block:

```text
Contemporary pochoir stencil and matte cut-paper collage illustration on warm unbleached paper (#fbf9f4). Visible paper cut edges, delicate drop shadows, stippled gouache textures, Monograph and Broadside color palette (terracotta, warm charcoal, slate teal, amber gold). Clean, welcoming editorial composition, completely wordless, zero text.

Characters featured:
- Bumpy: large pear-shaped blue-green gumdrop character with small rounded bumps, no band-aids, wearing round wire spectacles perched on his nose, cut-paper legs and bare feet, looking thoughtful and observant.
- Spring: lively coiled terracotta paper spring character with a single unified, contiguous vertical column of spiral coils topped with a smooth domed head featuring happy eyes and an open smile, paper legs and bare feet, no shoes, zero metal parts.
- Stacks: three smooth cut-paper stones stacked vertically (blue on top, yellow in middle, red on bottom), every single stone having its own distinct pair of eyes and mouth, one pair of legs with bare feet on bottom red stone, one pair of arms on middle yellow stone.
```
